Scope: the whole site (screener.html primary; index.html, report.html, insiders.html share the world). Mode: Operate (screener, insiders, report); the home page is the one Persuade surface inside the same world.

Audience and job: self-directed investors, newcomers and experienced alike, scanning small US companies that pass eight checks, then opening one to check why it scores what it does. Constraints: methodology, data, URLs and query params untouched; no invented figures; plain English, no en/em dashes; static HTML/CSS/ES modules.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every claim TFA makes sits beside the live figure that proves it. It refuses the dark fintech dashboard of rounded cards, neon accents and decorative charts.

OWN-WORLD: one cool neutral scale from navy-black to near-white, hairline 1px seams, 8px frames, an inverted near-white primary button; emerald and muted red appear only for a metric's sign; Archivo at two weights with true tabular figures, headings a little condensed, tickers in its semibold (changed from Geist after the detector flagged it as a category default).

STORY: The visitor reads one short claim (how many companies passed, why this one ranks), sees the proof beside it, opens a row to read its 100-point ledger, then goes to the full report.

FIRST VIEWPORT: on the screener, a one-line claim that carries its live counts ("77 of 4,229 companies pass all eight quality checks"), the data date in the header bar (in the claim block on phones, where the bar hides it), the toolbar, then the ranked table starting above the fold at 1280x800 with at least eight rows; each row shows its score as a numeral plus a segmented 100-point tape.

FORM: Claim and Proof (monochrome product marketing), a dealt challenger from the bolder re-roll; seed 419668f7. Signature interaction: the 100-point tape, ten segments sized to each component's maximum and filled to its earned points; opening a row unfolds its ledger in place.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
