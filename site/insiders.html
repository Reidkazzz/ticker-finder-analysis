Insider buying

Executives, directors and big owners buying their own company's stock on the open market, with their own money. Newest filings first, checked every weekday evening.

 

Tier 1 means $10 million or more bought on the open market in the last 30 days. Tier 2 means $1 million or more, or a single buyer growing their stake by 20% or more. Only real purchases count. Option exercises, stock awards and sales are left out, because insiders sell for many reasons but buy for one.

New 5%+ stakes

Schedule 13D filings: an investor crossed 5% ownership and may push the company for changes. Newest first.
